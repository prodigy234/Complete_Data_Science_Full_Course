Introduction & How To Use This Brief

This document is the task brief for a hands-on Tableau training exercise built around a fictional, training-only dataset that simulates Nestle Nigeria Plc. The companion workbook, Nestle_Nigeria_Training_Data.xlsx, contains 14 sheets: four master (reference) sheets and ten department sheets - Operations, Supply Chain, Legal, Admin, Customer Service, Information Technology, Accounts and Finance, Human Resources, Internal Control, and Risk Management and Compliance.

For every sheet, this brief gives you: (1) a full explanation of every column, (2) the specific data-preparation task to perform on that column inside Tableau, (3) the measures/calculated fields you must build, (4) the relationships you must create between sheets, (5) the KPIs to design, (6) the charts to build, and (7) the exact title to give the finished dashboard page.

Work through each department in order. Do not look at the second document ("Document 2 - Solutions Guide") until you have attempted every task - it contains the full worked solution, formulas and step-by-step build for everything listed here.

Data Model Overview (Master Sheets)

These four sheets form the reference ('dimension') backbone of the data model. Every department sheet links back to one or more of them using the key columns shown in bold below. Your first task in Tableau is always to inspect these sheets before touching a single department sheet.

Employee Master

The people dimension. Every EmployeeID used across the other nine department sheets (as a supervisor, approver, ticket owner, auditor, risk owner, etc.) is defined once here.

ColumnData TypeDescriptionTask To Perform In Tableau
EmployeeIDText, unique keyUnique staff identifier, format EMP0001.Set as a Text data type in Tableau (not a number), otherwise leading zeros are lost. Confirm there are no duplicate IDs.
FullNameTextEmployee's full name.Check for blank or inconsistent spacing/capitalisation; trim if needed.
DeptCodeText3-letter code linking the employee to a department.Validate every value exists in Department Master; use this field to build the relationship to department-level sheets.
DepartmentTextFull department name (descriptive copy of DeptCode).Use as the human-readable label on filters/legends instead of the raw code.
LocationIDTextSite where the employee is based.Validate against Location Master; use to build a Region/State geographic hierarchy.
LocationNameTextDescriptive site name.Use for tooltips and map labels.
GradeText (ordinal)Job grade/band.Create a custom sort order (Officer to Head of Section) since alphabetical sort is meaningless here.
GenderTextMale/Female.Use only for lawful, aggregate workforce-diversity reporting; never publish at individual level.
HireDateDateDate the employee joined.Create calculated fields for Tenure (DATEDIFF in years from HireDate to TODAY()) and Hire Year for cohort analysis.
EmploymentStatusTextActive or Exited.Use as a Boolean-style filter; combine with HireDate to compute attrition by year.

Department Master

The department dimension: one row per department, used to label DeptCode everywhere else and to hold department-level headcount.

ColumnData TypeDescriptionTask To Perform In Tableau
DeptCodeText, unique key3-letter department code (OPS, LEG, SCM, ADM, CS, IT, FIN, HR, IC, RMC).Relationship key to every fact sheet's DeptCode column.
Department NameTextFull department name.Use as the display label instead of the code in all visuals.
Department Head TitleTextTitle of the department's senior officer.Optional reference field for dashboard subtitles/context.
Headcount (Active)NumberCount of active employees in the department (pre-aggregated).Cross-check this static figure against a live COUNTD of Employee Master filtered to Active, to teach validation of pre-aggregated reference numbers.

Location Master

The geography dimension: Nestle Nigeria's 8 factories, offices and depots.

ColumnData TypeDescriptionTask To Perform In Tableau
LocationIDText, unique keySite code LOC01-LOC08.Relationship key to every fact sheet's LocationID column.
Location NameTextDescriptive site name.Use as map/legend label.
RegionTextGeopolitical zone (e.g. South West, North Central).Build a geographic hierarchy Region > State > Location Name for drill-down maps.
StateTextNigerian state.Assign a Geographic Role of 'State/Province' if you want native Tableau mapping; otherwise use as a filter only since these are fictional coordinates.
Site TypeTextFactory, Depot or Office.Use to filter Operations analysis to factories only, and Admin/Legal analysis across all site types.

Product Master

The product dimension: 8 flagship Nestle Nigeria brands referenced by Operations, Supply Chain, Customer Service and Finance.

ColumnData TypeDescriptionTask To Perform In Tableau

Operations - Tasks

Source sheet: OperationsDepartment code: OPS

Task A - Column Explanation & Column-Level Tasks

#ColumnData TypeDescriptionTask To Perform On This Column
1ProductionIDText, unique keyUnique identifier for one production run/record.Confirm uniqueness; set as a dimension, never aggregate it.
2DateDateDate of the production run.Create Year, Quarter and Month calculated/date-part fields for trend analysis; build a Date hierarchy.
3LocationIDText, foreign keyFactory where production happened (LOC02 or LOC03).Build relationship to Location Master; use LocationName in visuals instead of the raw code.
4ProductIDText, foreign keyProduct manufactured in the run.Build relationship to Product Master; use Product Name/Category for grouping.
5ProductionLineTextLine 1 to Line 6.Use as a dimension on rows for a line-by-line OEE comparison.
6ShiftTextMorning, Afternoon or Night.Create a custom sort (Morning, Afternoon, Night) for a clean small-multiple chart.
7DeptCodeText, foreign keyAlways OPS.Relationship key to Department Master; mainly used when blending Operations with other departments' spend.
8SupervisorEmployeeIDText, foreign keyEmployee ID of the shift supervisor.Relationship to Employee Master to show supervisor name and to analyse output by supervisor.
9PlannedOutputCartonsNumber (measure)Target output for the run.Aggregate with SUM; this is the denominator of Output Achievement %.
10ActualOutputCartonsNumber (measure)Actual cartons produced.Aggregate with SUM; numerator of Output Achievement % and OEE.
11DowntimeHoursNumber (measure)Hours the line was down during the run.Aggregate with SUM/AVG; drives the Downtime Pareto chart.
12DowntimeReasonTextReason for downtime, or 'None'.Filter out 'None' before building the Pareto chart of loss reasons.
13ScrapQtyCartonsNumber (measure)Cartons scrapped/rejected.Used to compute Scrap Rate % as a calculated field.
14OEE_PercentNumber (measure, formula-driven)Actual/Planned output ratio calculated in the source data.Cross-check against a Tableau-native calculated field SUM(ActualOutputCartons)/SUM(PlannedOutputCartons) to confirm they match.

Task B - Measures & Calculated-Field Tasks

  • Create calculated field 'Output Achievement %' = SUM([ActualOutputCartons]) / SUM([PlannedOutputCartons]).
  • Create calculated field 'Scrap Rate %' = SUM([ScrapQtyCartons]) / SUM([ActualOutputCartons]).
  • Create calculated field 'Total Downtime Hours' = SUM([DowntimeHours]) with a table calc for % of total.
  • Create a Date hierarchy (Year > Quarter > Month) on the Date field for drill-down trend charts.
  • Build the relationship OPS.LocationID -> Location Master.LocationID and OPS.ProductID -> Product Master.ProductID.

Task C - Relationship-Building Task

Build the relationship(s) between the Operations sheet and the relevant master sheet(s) using the foreign-key columns identified in Task A above (look for columns marked 'foreign key'). Confirm the relationship cardinality is many-to-one (many Operations rows to one master record) and that no orphan keys exist.

  • Create calculated field 'Average Lead Time (Days)' = AVG([LeadTimeDays]).
  • Build the relationship SCM.Supplier as a stand-alone dimension (no master sheet needed) but relate SCM.ProductID/LocationID to their master sheets.
  • Group suppliers with inconsistent naming (if any) using a Tableau Group before building the treemap.

  • Create calculated field 'Avg Days to Close' = AVG(IIF([Status]="Closed",DATEDIFF('day',[DateOpened],[DateClosed]),NULL)).
  • Build the relationship Legal.DeptCodeInvolved -> Department Master.DeptCode and Legal.AssignedEmployeeID -> Employee Master.EmployeeID.
