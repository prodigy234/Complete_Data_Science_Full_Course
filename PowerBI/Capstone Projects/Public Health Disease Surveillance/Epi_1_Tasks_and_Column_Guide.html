1. How to Use This File

This is the problem brief for the Public Health & Disease Surveillance Power BI training project. It does not contain the answers - it tells you what the dataset contains, what every column means, which relationships you must build in the Model view, which DAX measures you must write, and exactly what each of the five dashboard pages must contain. Work through it in order: understand the columns first, then build the model, then the DAX measures, then the dashboard pages. A separate file, the Solutions Walkthrough, shows the fully worked solution end to end once you have attempted each task yourself.

Column Guide - Dim_Geography

The geographic backbone of the whole model, and the single feature that separates this project from a purely operational dataset: because it carries a Population figure, it is what allows a true epidemiological Incidence Rate per 100,000 measure rather than a raw case count. It is referenced from four different fact tables.

ColumnData TypeWhat it means / your task
GeographyIDText (Key)Unique identifier such as GEO001. Primary key of Dim_Geography, referenced by Fact_CaseReports[ResidenceGeographyID], Fact_Outbreaks[EpicenterGeographyID], Fact_ContactTracing[ResidenceGeographyID] and Fact_EnvironmentalSurveillance[GeographyID].
StateTextNigerian state. Used to roll many LGAs up into a higher-level geographic view on state-level maps and bar charts.
LGATextLocal Government Area, the primary reporting unit for Nigerian disease surveillance in real practice. This is the field placed on the most granular geographic visuals.
RegionTextGeopolitical zone (South West, South South, South East, North West, North Central, North East) rolled up from State, giving a national-level regional view.
PopulationWhole NumberApproximate LGA population. The denominator in every Incidence Rate per 100,000 measure; summed (not averaged) whenever more than one geography is in the current filter context.
AreaKm2Whole NumberApproximate land area in square kilometres. Used together with Population to derive PopulationDensityPerKm2.
PopulationDensityPerKm2Decimal NumberPopulation divided by AreaKm2, pre-calculated. Useful for exploring whether densely populated LGAs show different disease patterns than sparsely populated ones.
UrbanRuralClassificationTextUrban, Semi-Urban or Rural, derived from population density. A key field for an urban-versus-rural disease burden comparison.
HealthZoneTextA local health-system zoning label used for administrative grouping of LGAs within a state. Descriptive detail rather than a primary analytical field.

Column Guide - Dim_Diseases

Describes every disease under surveillance. Every case report, outbreak and (indirectly, through TargetDisease) vaccine in the model traces back to a disease defined here.

ColumnData TypeWhat it means / your task
DiseaseIDText (Key)Unique identifier such as DIS001. Primary key of Dim_Diseases, referenced by Fact_CaseReports[DiseaseID] and Fact_Outbreaks[DiseaseID].
DiseaseNameTextThe disease's common name, for example Malaria, Cholera or Lassa Fever. The field placed on most disease-level charts such as Top Diseases by Case Count.
DiseaseCategoryTextVector-borne, Waterborne, Airborne/Respiratory, Vaccine-Preventable, Zoonotic, Viral Haemorrhagic Fever or Other Notifiable. Used to roll individual diseases up into a manageable number of groups for category-level charts.
ICD10CodeTextThe disease's real-world ICD-10 classification code, included so students can practise combining a code and a name into a single label using a calculated column.
TransmissionModeTextHow the disease spreads, for example 'Mosquito bite (Anopheles)' or 'Respiratory droplets'. Descriptive detail useful for tooltip content rather than aggregate charting.
IncubationPeriodDaysMinWhole NumberThe shortest typical incubation period in days. Reference information for outbreak investigation, not aggregated directly.
IncubationPeriodDaysMaxWhole NumberThe longest typical incubation period in days. Used together with the minimum to define the exposure window investigated during contact tracing.
ReferenceCaseFatalityRatePercentDecimal NumberA published reference case fatality rate for the disease, included so students can compare this textbook figure against the Case Fatality Rate % measure actually calculated from this dataset's case outcomes.
IsNotifiableText (Yes/No)Flags whether the disease is legally notifiable. All 30 diseases in this dataset are notifiable, since the case reporting system only captures notifiable conditions, but the column is retained for completeness and for filtering logic that mirrors real surveillance systems.
IsOutbreakProneText (Yes/No)Flags diseases capable of generating a declared outbreak. Only outbreak-prone diseases can appear in Fact_Outbreaks, which is enforced at data-generation time and worth confirming as a data-quality check.
IsVaccinePreventableText (Yes/No)Flags diseases with a corresponding vaccine in Dim_Vaccines[TargetDisease]. Useful for a vaccine-preventable-disease-burden visual on the immunization dashboard page.

Column Guide - Dim_HealthFacilities

Describes every facility that can report a case, run a lab test, or administer a vaccine. It is referenced from three different fact tables, and one of those relationships (to Fact_LabResults) must be modelled as inactive, making this table central to the project's most advanced DAX teaching moment.

ColumnData TypeWhat it means / your task
FacilityIDText (Key)Unique identifier such as FAC001. Primary key of Dim_HealthFacilities, referenced by Fact_CaseReports[ReportingFacilityID], Fact_Immunizations[FacilityID] and Fact_LabResults[TestingFacilityID].
FacilityNameTextThe facility's display name, used as the axis on facility-level visuals.
FacilityTypeTextPrimary Health Centre, General Hospital, Teaching Hospital, Private Clinic, Isolation Centre or Diagnostic Laboratory.
FacilityLevelTextPrimary, Secondary or Tertiary, derived from FacilityType. Useful for comparing surveillance performance across different tiers of the health system.
OwnershipTypeTextPublic, Private, Faith-Based or NGO-Supported. Useful context for a sector-comparison discussion.
GeographyIDText (Foreign Key)Links the facility to its LGA via Dim_Geography[GeographyID]. This is the foreign key that creates a snowflake relationship Dim_Geography (one) to Dim_HealthFacilities (many).
ReportingStatusText (Active/Inactive)Flags whether the facility is currently submitting surveillance reports. A small governance field useful for a reporting-completeness discussion.
HasLabCapacityText (Yes/No)Flags whether the facility can process laboratory specimens on-site. Only facilities flagged Yes appear as a TestingFacilityID in Fact_LabResults.
DateEstablishedDateThe date the facility opened. Can support a simple health-system-growth analysis over time.
ContactPhoneTextFacility contact phone number. Included for realism only, not for analysis; a good candidate to hide in report view.

RecommendedAgeGroupTextThe age group the vaccine is recommended for, for example '9-15 Months' or '18 Years+'. Useful context alongside Fact_Immunizations[AgeGroupTargeted].

Column Guide - Dim_Patients

Describes every individual who has ever been the subject of a case report, without carrying any personally identifying information. Deliberately smaller than the number of case reports, since some individuals (particularly for recurring illnesses like malaria) are reported more than once across the seven-year history.

ColumnData TypeWhat it means / your task
PatientRefIDText (Key)Unique de-identified reference such as CS000001. Primary key of Dim_Patients and the field used to relate to Fact_CaseReports[PatientRefID].
GenderTextMale or Female. A core demographic segmentation field.
DateOfBirthDateDate of birth, the source for the calculated AgeAtReport field.
AgeAtReportWhole NumberPre-computed age in years as at 2026. Used to build age-band groupings that feed demographic disease-burden visuals.
RiskGroupCategoryTextGeneral Population, Under-5 Child, Pregnant Woman, Health Worker, Elderly (60+) or Immunocompromised. A key epidemiological field: several notifiable diseases are tracked specifically by risk group, for example maternal and neonatal conditions or health-worker occupational exposure.
OccupationCategoryTextThe case-subject's occupation category. Useful for an occupational-exposure analysis, for example among Health Workers.
PregnancyStatusTextPregnant, Not Pregnant or N/A. Populated only for females of reproductive age; N/A for males and for females outside that age range.
ResidenceGeographyIDText (Foreign Key)Links the case-subject to their LGA of residence via Dim_Geography[GeographyID]. Note that Fact_CaseReports also carries its own ResidenceGeographyID directly, copied from this table at report time, so that a case's residence remains fixed even if this dimension is later updated.

Column Guide - Dim_Date

A dedicated, contiguous calendar table is mandatory best practice in Power BI, and is what allows time intelligence DAX functions (TOTALYTD, SAMEPERIODLASTYEAR, DATEADD and similar) to work correctly. This is the longest date table of any project in this training series, seven full years, giving genuinely meaningful multi-year epidemic-cycle analysis, and it carries a real epidemiological week number alongside the standard calendar fields.

ColumnData TypeWhat it means / your task
DateDate (Key)The calendar date itself, one row per day with no gaps. Primary key of Dim_Date; must be marked as a Date Table in Power BI (Table Tools > Mark as Date Table) using this column.
DayWhole NumberDay of the month (1-31).
DayNameTextFull weekday name (Monday, Tuesday and so on).
MonthNumberWhole NumberNumeric month (1-12). Used to sort MonthName correctly on the X-axis, since Power BI sorts text columns alphabetically unless a Sort By Column is set.
MonthNameTextFull month name. Placed on the axis of every monthly trend chart in the project; must have Sort By Column set to MonthNumber.
QuarterWhole NumberCalendar quarter (1-4).
HalfYearTextH1 or H2, flagging the half of the calendar year.
YearWhole NumberCalendar year (2019 through 2025 in this dataset). The field most commonly placed in a slicer at the top of every dashboard page, and what makes true year-over-year DAX comparisons possible.
WeekdayWeekendTextFlags each date as Weekday or Weekend.
FiscalYearTextFiscal year label such as FY2023, mirroring the calendar year in this dataset.
IsPublicHolidayText (Yes/No)Flags a small set of fixed-date Nigerian public holidays.
ISOWeekNumberWhole NumberThe ISO 8601 week number (1-53) for the date, the numeric basis of the epidemiological week.
EpiWeekLabelTextA combined 'YYYY-Www' label such as '2024-W17', the standard way epidemiologists group and compare case counts week over week. This is the field an epi curve should ideally be built on for genuinely authentic surveillance reporting, though this project's core trend visuals use MonthName for simplicity and introduces EpiWeekLabel as an extension exercise.

Column Guide - Fact_Outbreaks

Records every formally declared outbreak. It sits upstream of Fact_CaseReports in the sense that a case report can optionally be linked back to the outbreak it was part of, making this the smallest fact table in the model but an important 'one' side of a fact-to-fact relationship.

ColumnData TypeWhat it means / your task
OutbreakIDText (Key)Unique identifier such as OUT0001. Primary key of Fact_Outbreaks and the 'one' side of the fact-to-fact relationship to Fact_CaseReports[OutbreakID].
DiseaseIDText (Foreign Key)Links the outbreak to Dim_Diseases[DiseaseID], restricted in this dataset to diseases flagged IsOutbreakProne = Yes.
EpicenterGeographyIDText (Foreign Key)Links the outbreak to the LGA where it was first detected, via Dim_Geography[GeographyID].
DateDeclaredDateThe date the outbreak was formally declared.
DateClosedDate (nullable)The date the outbreak was formally closed, blank for outbreaks still flagged Active. Any measure averaging outbreak duration must explicitly exclude blank values, a useful blank-handling lesson.
OutbreakStatusTextActive, Contained or Closed. Drives the Total Active Outbreaks measure and the outbreak status donut chart.
SeverityTextLow, Moderate, High or Critical, assigned at declaration based on early case counts and disease characteristics.
ResponseTeamLeadOfficerIDText (Foreign Key)Links to the Response Team Lead in Dim_SurveillanceOfficers who coordinated the outbreak response.
TotalCasesAtClosureWhole NumberThe officially reported total case count for the outbreak, which may differ slightly from a live COUNTROWS of linked Fact_CaseReports rows, since not every case generated during an outbreak is always formally linked back to it in real surveillance systems, a deliberate and realistic data-quality nuance.
TotalDeathsAtClosureWhole NumberThe officially reported total death count for the outbreak. Used to compute an outbreak-level case fatality rate distinct from the case-report-level Case Fatality Rate % measure.

Column Guide - Fact_CaseReports

The central fact table of the entire model. Almost every DAX measure in this project either aggregates a column from Fact_CaseReports directly or counts rows in it, and three other fact tables (Lab Results, Contact Tracing, and optionally Outbreaks) hang off it.

ColumnData TypeWhat it means / your task
CaseIDText (Key)Unique identifier for the case report, formatted CASE000001. Primary key of Fact_CaseReports and the 'one' side of the fact-to-fact relationships to Fact_LabResults[CaseID] and Fact_ContactTracing[IndexCaseID].
DiseaseIDText (Foreign Key)Links the case to Dim_Diseases[DiseaseID]. Every disease-burden visual in the project depends on this column.
PatientRefIDText (Foreign Key)Links the case to the case-subject in Dim_Patients[PatientRefID]. Many case reports can point to the same patient over the seven-year history, which is what makes this a genuine one-to-many relationship.
ReportingFacilityIDText (Foreign Key)Links the case to the facility that reported it, via Dim_HealthFacilities[FacilityID].
ReportedByOfficerIDText (Foreign Key)Links the case to the surveillance officer who reported it, via Dim_SurveillanceOfficers[OfficerID].
OutbreakIDText (Foreign Key, optional)Links the case to a declared outbreak in Fact_Outbreaks, populated for roughly 30% of cases involving an outbreak-prone disease and left blank for all other cases. This optional relationship is central to the Outbreak-Linked Cases % measure and to a blank-handling lesson using ISBLANK.
ResidenceGeographyIDText (Foreign Key)Links the case to the case-subject's LGA of residence, via Dim_Geography[GeographyID]. This is the field behind every Incidence Rate per 100,000 measure.
OnsetDateDateThe date symptoms began. This is the column that should carry the ACTIVE relationship to Dim_Date[Date], since a true epidemic curve is built on onset date, not the date the case happened to be reported.
ReportDateDateThe date the case was formally reported into the surveillance system. This column should carry an INACTIVE relationship to Dim_Date[Date], activated only inside specific measures with USERELATIONSHIP when a reporting-timeliness view is genuinely needed.
ReportingDelayDaysWhole NumberReportDate minus OnsetDate, pre-calculated in days. The core numeric field behind the Average Reporting Delay and Timely Reporting % measures, both important surveillance-system-performance indicators.
CaseClassificationTextConfirmed, Probable, Suspected or Discarded, following standard case-definition terminology. Drives the Confirmed Cases measure and the case classification donut chart.
CaseOutcomeTextRecovered, Under Treatment, Referred, Lost to Follow-up, or Died. The field the Case Fatality Rate % measure filters on, and one of the most sensitive and important fields in the whole dataset.
HospitalizationStatusText (Yes/No)Flags whether the case required hospitalization. Drives the Hospitalization Rate % measure.
IsOutbreakRelatedText (Yes/No)A convenience flag mirroring whether OutbreakID is populated, included so students can compare a text-flag approach against an ISBLANK-on-the-key approach for the same underlying question.
SourceOfReportTextHealth Facility, Community-Based Surveillance, Laboratory, Death Registration, or Event-Based Surveillance. Useful for a surveillance-channel-mix visual.

Column Guide - Fact_LabResults

Records laboratory confirmation testing. Around 72% of case reports generate at least one lab result, and a minority generate two, modelling real-world repeat or confirmatory testing. This is the fact table behind the entire Laboratory & Diagnostics dashboard page, and the table where the project's most advanced USERELATIONSHIP example lives.

ColumnData TypeWhat it means / your task
LabResultIDText (Key)Unique identifier for the test, formatted LAB000001. Primary key of Fact_LabResults.
CaseIDText (Foreign Key)Links the test back to Fact_CaseReports[CaseID]. This fact-to-fact relationship is one-to-many because a single case can generate more than one lab test.
SpecimenTypeTextBlood, Stool, Throat Swab, Nasopharyngeal Swab, Cerebrospinal Fluid, Urine or Skin Lesion Swab. Descriptive detail useful for a specimen-type-mix visual.
TestTypeTextRDT (Rapid Diagnostic Test), PCR, ELISA, Culture and Sensitivity, Microscopy, or Serology. Useful for comparing test-method mix across diseases.
SpecimenCollectionDateDateThe date the specimen was collected from the patient.
ResultDateDateThe date the result was released. SpecimenCollectionDate and ResultDate together define TurnaroundTimeDays.
TestResultTextPositive, Negative, Indeterminate, or Pending. Drives the Lab Positivity Rate % measure and the test result donut chart.
TestingFacilityIDText (Foreign Key)Links the test to the laboratory that actually processed it, via Dim_HealthFacilities[FacilityID], restricted to facilities flagged HasLabCapacity = Yes. Because Dim_HealthFacilities already has an active relationship to Fact_CaseReports[ReportingFacilityID], and Fact_CaseReports already relates to Fact_LabResults, this direct relationship must be created INACTIVE to avoid a closed loop of active relationships, and activated with USERELATIONSHIP only inside measures that specifically need the testing facility's own perspective rather than the reporting facility's.
TurnaroundTimeDaysWhole NumberResultDate minus SpecimenCollectionDate, pre-calculated in days. The core numeric field behind the Average Lab Turnaround Time measure, an important laboratory-system-performance indicator.

Column Guide - Fact_Immunizations

Records vaccination activity at the session level (a batch of doses given at a facility on a given day for a given vaccine and age group) rather than the individual-dose level, which mirrors how most real-world routine immunization data is actually aggregated and reported. It drives the entire Immunization & Vaccine-Preventable Disease dashboard page.

ColumnData TypeWhat it means / your task
ImmunizationIDText (Key)Unique identifier for the session, formatted IMM000001. Primary key of Fact_Immunizations.
VaccineIDText (Foreign Key)Links the session to Dim_Vaccines[VaccineID].
FacilityIDText (Foreign Key)Links the session to the facility it was conducted at, via Dim_HealthFacilities[FacilityID].
VaccinationDateDateThe date of the immunization session. Carries an active relationship to Dim_Date[Date].
DoseNumberWhole NumberWhich dose in the series this session represents (1 through 4, depending on the vaccine's DosesInSeries).
AgeGroupTargetedTextThe age group vaccinated in this session, for example '0-11 Months' or '18 Years+'.

DosesAdministeredWhole NumberNumber of doses given in this session. Summed to build the Total Doses Administered measure, the primary immunization-coverage-proxy KPI.
DosesWastedWhole NumberNumber of doses opened but not administered (spoiled, expired in the open vial, or damaged) in this session. The numerator of the Vaccine Wastage Rate % measure, an important vaccine-supply-chain KPI.

Column Guide - Fact_ContactTracing

Records every contact identified and monitored following a case report, for roughly 28% of all cases (contact tracing is typically only initiated for a subset of higher-priority diseases and cases in real practice). This is the largest fact table in the model and, along with the two relationships it forces to be inactive, one of the richest single tables for teaching filter propagation through a fact-to-fact chain.

ColumnData TypeWhat it means / your task
ContactTracingIDText (Key)Unique identifier for the traced contact, formatted CT000001. Primary key of Fact_ContactTracing.
IndexCaseIDText (Foreign Key)Links the contact back to the case they were exposed to, via Fact_CaseReports[CaseID]. This fact-to-fact relationship is one-to-many because a single index case commonly generates several contacts (household members, co-workers, healthcare workers and so on).
RelationshipToCaseTextHousehold Member, Healthcare Worker, Community Contact, Travel Companion, or Co-worker. Useful for understanding transmission pathways.
ResidenceGeographyIDText (Foreign Key)Links the contact to their LGA of residence, via Dim_Geography[GeographyID]. Because Dim_Geography already reaches Fact_ContactTracing indirectly through Fact_CaseReports[ResidenceGeographyID] followed by the CaseID relationship, this direct relationship must be created INACTIVE to avoid a closed loop, and activated with USERELATIONSHIP only when a contact's own residence (rather than the index case's residence) is specifically needed.
MonitoringStartDateDateThe date daily monitoring of the contact began. Because Dim_Date already reaches Fact_ContactTracing indirectly through Fact_CaseReports[OnsetDate] followed by the CaseID relationship, this direct relationship must ALSO be created INACTIVE, for exactly the same closed-loop reason as ResidenceGeographyID above; this table is the clearest single example in the project of a fact table needing more than one inactive relationship at once.
MonitoringEndDateDateThe date monitoring concluded, either because the observation period ended or the contact's status changed. Used with MonitoringStartDate to calculate DaysMonitored.
ContactStatusTextCompleted - No Symptoms, Under Monitoring, Became a Case, or Lost to Follow-up. Drives the Contacts Converted to Cases % measure, an important secondary-attack-rate-style indicator.
DevelopedSymptomsText (Yes/No)Flags whether the contact developed symptoms during monitoring, closely related to but not perfectly identical to ContactStatus, giving students a small data-consistency check to reason through.
DaysMonitoredWhole NumberMonitoringEndDate minus MonitoringStartDate, pre-calculated in days.

Captures the non-human, environmental side of disease surveillance: mosquito vector indices, water quality testing, sanitation inspections and rodent surveillance. This table is not fact-to-fact linked to any other fact table, making all three of its dimension relationships safely active, a useful contrast to the more constrained relationships on Fact_LabResults and Fact_ContactTracing.

ColumnData TypeWhat it means / your task

GeographyIDText (Foreign Key)Links the activity to the LGA it was conducted in, via Dim_Geography[GeographyID].
SurveillanceTypeTextVector Surveillance - Mosquito, Water Quality Testing, Sanitation Inspection, or Rodent Surveillance. Drives the surveillance-type breakdown visual.
SurveillanceDateDateThe date the activity was conducted. Carries an active relationship to Dim_Date[Date].
FindingResultTextPositive/Negative for vector and rodent surveillance, or Satisfactory/Unsatisfactory for water quality and sanitation. Drives the Vector Surveillance Positivity % measure.
IndexValueDecimal NumberA numeric index specific to the surveillance type, for example a larval or vector index for mosquito surveillance, or a water quality score. Not directly comparable across different SurveillanceType values, an important caveat worth teaching students before they average this column blindly.
ConductedByOfficerIDText (Foreign Key)Links the activity to the surveillance officer who conducted it, via Dim_SurveillanceOfficers[OfficerID].

4. DAX Measures To Be Built

Build the following measures, in this order, inside a dedicated Measures table. For each one, the goal (why it exists) is given - your task is to write the DAX yourself before checking the Solutions file.

Measure NameUsed OnYour Task (goal of the measure)
Total Cases ReportedUsed on every pageThe foundation KPI of the entire report; almost every other measure either builds on it or is filtered the same way it is.
Confirmed CasesExecutive OverviewDistinguishes laboratory- or clinically-confirmed disease burden from the broader Suspected and Probable case pool, an important distinction in every surveillance bulletin.
Case Fatality Rate %Executive Overview, Disease & Outbreak SurveillanceOne of the most closely watched indicators in any disease surveillance bulletin, and one that must be presented carefully given its sensitivity.
Incidence Rate per 100,000Executive Overview, Disease & Outbreak SurveillanceThe single most authentic epidemiological measure in this project: a raw case count is close to meaningless without knowing the size of the population it came from, since a populous LGA will always report more cases than a sparsely populated one even if the underlying risk is identical.
