1. How to Use This File

This is the problem brief for the Pharmacy & Medication Supply Chain Power BI training project. It does not contain the answers - it tells you what the dataset contains, what every column means, which relationships you must build in the Model view, which DAX measures you must write, and exactly what each of the five dashboard pages must contain. Work through it in order: understand the columns first, then build the model, then the DAX measures, then the dashboard pages. A separate file, the Solutions Walkthrough, shows the fully worked solution end to end once you have attempted each task yourself.

2. Dataset Overview

The workbook Pharmacy_Supply_Chain_Dataset.xlsx contains 11 related tables built as a star/snowflake schema: 6 dimension tables and 5 fact tables, spanning five years of data (2021-2025). Study the table below before opening Power BI.

Column Guide - Dim_Medications

Describes every medication the pharmacy business stocks, sells and reorders. It is the single most heavily used dimension in the model, referenced directly by all five fact tables (Purchase Orders, Stock Receipts, Sales, Transfers and Returns).

ColumnData TypeWhat it means / your task
MedicationIDText (Key)Unique identifier such as MED001. Primary key of Dim_Medications and the field used to relate to every fact table's MedicationID column.
MedicationNameTextThe commercial/dosage-specific name, for example 'Paracetamol 500mg'. This is the field placed on most medication-level charts such as Top Selling Medications.
GenericNameTextThe underlying generic drug name, for example Paracetamol without the strength. Useful for grouping different strengths or forms of the same active ingredient together.
CategoryTextTherapeutic category such as Analgesic, Antibiotic, Antimalarial, Antihypertensive or Vaccine. Used to roll dozens of individual medications up into a manageable number of groups for category-level charts.
DosageFormTextTablet, Capsule, Syrup, Injection, Cream, Lotion, Inhaler, IV Bag or Sachet. Useful for a dispensing-format analysis and for filtering out non-oral forms in certain views.
StrengthPerUnitDecimal NumberThe numeric strength value, for example 500 for a 500mg tablet. Combine with StrengthUnit for a full label; not typically aggregated on its own.
StrengthUnitTextThe unit that StrengthPerUnit is measured in, for example mg, IU or %. A descriptive attribute rather than an analytical one.
PackSizeWhole NumberNumber of units in a standard pack (10, 20, 30 and so on). Used to convert unit-level quantities into pack-level reorder recommendations.
UnitCostNGNCurrency (Whole Number)The pharmacy's cost to acquire one unit of the medication from a supplier, in Naira. Central to gross margin calculations and to the value of every stock movement.
UnitSellingPriceNGNCurrency (Whole Number)The retail price charged per unit at the point of sale. Set at a markup over UnitCostNGN and used throughout the Sales & Revenue dashboard page.
RequiresPrescriptionText (Yes/No)Flags whether the medication is a prescription-only medicine (POM) or an over-the-counter (OTC) product. Drives the Prescription Sales % measure and a compliance-style visual.
IsControlledSubstanceText (Yes/No)Flags medications subject to stricter dispensing control (for example Tramadol, Diazepam). A small but important field for a pharmacy compliance discussion.
ShelfLifeMonthsWhole NumberTypical shelf life of the medication once manufactured. Used alongside Fact_StockReceipts[ExpiryDate] to reason about expiry risk.
ATCCodeTextA short fictional Anatomical Therapeutic Chemical-style classification code, included so students can practise combining a code and a name into a single label using a calculated column.
ReorderLevelUnitsWhole NumberThe stock quantity threshold below which the medication should be reordered. Used as a reference line on inventory visuals and in a simple 'Below Reorder Level' flag measure.

ColumnData TypeWhat it means / your task

SupplierNameTextThe supplier's registered business name, used as the axis on the Total Spend by Supplier chart and the supplier scorecard table.
SupplierTypeTextLocal Distributor, Manufacturer or Importer. Useful for comparing cost and lead-time patterns across different supplier categories.
CountryTextCountry the supplier is based in. Mostly Nigeria, with a minority of importers based abroad, giving students a small international-sourcing story to explore.
CityTextCity the supplier operates from. A descriptive attribute rather than a primary analytical field.
ContactPersonTextThe supplier's named contact. Included for realism and drillthrough detail only.
PhoneTextSupplier contact phone number. Should be hidden from report view; included purely for operational realism.
EmailTextSupplier contact email. Same treatment as Phone - realistic but not analytical.
PaymentTermsTextThe supplier's standard payment terms (Net 30, Net 45, Net 60, Net 90 or Cash on Delivery). Useful context alongside Fact_PurchaseOrders[PaymentStatus] for a cash-flow discussion.
LeadTimeDaysWhole NumberThe supplier's stated standard delivery lead time in days. Compared against the actual gap between OrderDate and ExpectedDeliveryDate, and later against actual receipt dates, to assess reliability.
RatingDecimal Number (1-5)An overall supplier quality rating maintained by the procurement team. Used on the supplier scorecard table alongside the calculated On-Time Delivery % measure.
IsActiveText (Yes/No)Flags whether the supplier is currently approved for new orders. A small governance field useful for filtering out discontinued suppliers from forward-looking analysis.

Column Guide - Dim_Branches

Describes every physical location in the network. Uniquely in this model, Dim_Branches plays a role-playing, double role on Fact_StockTransfers (as both the sending and receiving branch), which is one of the central DAX teaching moments of this project.

ColumnData TypeWhat it means / your task
BranchIDText (Key)Unique identifier such as BR01. Primary key of Dim_Branches, referenced by Fact_PurchaseOrders, Fact_Sales, Fact_Returns, and twice by Fact_StockTransfers (FromBranchID and ToBranchID).
BranchNameTextThe branch's display name, used as the axis on most branch-level visuals.
BranchTypeTextRetail Pharmacy, Hospital Pharmacy or Distribution Warehouse. The three warehouse locations are where most purchase orders are received before stock is transferred out to retail and hospital branches.
CityTextCity the branch is located in.
StateTextNigerian state the branch is located in. Used for a geographic Sales by State visual.
RegionTextA broader geopolitical zone grouping (South West, South South, South East, North West, North Central) rolled up from State, giving a higher-level geographic view.
ManagerNameTextName of the branch manager. Useful for drillthrough detail rather than aggregate visuals.
OpeningDateDateThe date the branch opened. Can support a simple network-growth analysis over the five-year history.
StorageCapacityUnitsWhole NumberApproximate storage capacity in stock units. Warehouses have much larger capacity than retail branches, useful context for a utilisation-style discussion even though this dataset does not simulate live stock-on-hand.
ContactPhoneTextBranch contact phone number. Included for realism only, not for analysis.

ColumnData TypeWhat it means / your task
EmployeeIDText (Key)Unique identifier such as EMP0001. Primary key of Dim_Employees, referenced by Fact_PurchaseOrders[ProcurementOfficerID], Fact_Sales[DispensingEmployeeID] and Fact_StockTransfers[ApprovedByEmployeeID].
FirstNameTextEmployee's given name.
LastNameTextEmployee's family name. Combine with FirstName in a calculated column for a clean display name.
GenderTextEmployee's gender, usable for a simple workforce composition visual.
RoleTextPharmacist, Pharmacy Technician, Store Manager, Procurement Officer or Inventory Officer. Determines which fact tables an employee is realistically likely to appear on (for example, only Procurement Officers place purchase orders in this dataset).
BranchIDText (Foreign Key)Links each employee to their home branch via Dim_Branches[BranchID]. This is the foreign key that creates a snowflake relationship Dim_Branches (one) to Dim_Employees (many).
YearsOfExperienceWhole NumberNumber of years the employee has worked in pharmacy practice. Used on a scatter chart comparing experience against sales volume handled.
DateHiredDateThe date the employee joined the company. Can support a simple headcount-growth analysis.
LicenseNumberTextA fictional PCN-format license number for Pharmacists and Pharmacy Technicians, blank for other roles. Included for realism and drillthrough detail.
EmploymentTypeTextFull-Time, Part-Time or Locum. Useful for a workforce-composition visual on the branch and employee performance page.

Column Guide - Dim_Date

A dedicated, contiguous calendar table is mandatory best practice in Power BI, and is what allows time intelligence DAX functions (TOTALYTD, SAMEPERIODLASTYEAR, DATEADD and similar) to work correctly. In this model it relates to four different fact tables, each through its own natural date column.

ColumnData TypeWhat it means / your task
DateDate (Key)The calendar date itself, one row per day with no gaps. Primary key of Dim_Date; must be marked as a Date Table in Power BI (Table Tools > Mark as Date Table) using this column.
DayWhole NumberDay of the month (1-31). Rarely used alone but occasionally useful for day-of-month seasonality checks.
DayNameTextFull weekday name (Monday, Tuesday and so on). Used together with WeekdayWeekend to study whether sales volume differs on weekends.
MonthNumberWhole NumberNumeric month (1-12). Used to sort MonthName correctly on the X-axis, since Power BI sorts text columns alphabetically unless a Sort By Column is set.
MonthNameTextFull month name (January, February and so on). This is the field placed on the axis of every monthly trend chart in the project; it must have Sort By Column set to MonthNumber.
QuarterWhole NumberCalendar quarter (1-4). Used to build quarterly cards and slicers for executives who prefer a higher-level time view.
HalfYearTextH1 or H2, flagging whether the date falls in the first or second half of the calendar year.
YearWhole NumberCalendar year (2021 through 2025 in this dataset). The field most commonly placed in a slicer at the top of every dashboard page, and what makes true year-over-year DAX comparisons possible.
WeekdayWeekendTextFlags each date as Weekday or Weekend. Supports a simple DAX measure comparing average sales on weekdays versus weekends.
FiscalYearTextFiscal year label such as FY2023. Included so students can see how a fiscal calendar would be added even though it mirrors the calendar year in this dataset.
IsPublicHolidayText (Yes/No)Flags a small set of fixed-date Nigerian public holidays. Gives students a real field to test whether sales patterns differ on public holidays.

Column Guide - Fact_PurchaseOrders

The starting point of the supply chain: every unit of stock that eventually reaches a branch begins as a purchase order line here. It is the central fact table behind the Procurement & Supplier Performance dashboard page.

ColumnData TypeWhat it means / your task
POIDText (Key)Unique identifier for the order line, formatted PO000001. Primary key of Fact_PurchaseOrders and the 'one' side of the fact-to-fact relationship to Fact_StockReceipts[POID].
SupplierIDText (Foreign Key)Links the order to Dim_Suppliers[SupplierID]. Every supplier-performance visual depends on this column.
BranchIDText (Foreign Key)Links the order to the receiving location in Dim_Branches[BranchID]. Roughly 70% of orders are placed against one of the three distribution warehouses, reflecting a realistic centralised-procurement pattern.
MedicationIDText (Foreign Key)Links the order to Dim_Medications[MedicationID]. Each purchase order line is for a single medication SKU.
ProcurementOfficerIDText (Foreign Key)Links the order to the employee in Dim_Employees who placed it, filtered in this dataset to staff with the Procurement Officer role.
OrderDateDateThe date the order was placed. This is the column that should carry the ACTIVE relationship to Dim_Date[Date], since most procurement reporting is naturally order-date based.
ExpectedDeliveryDateDateOrderDate plus the supplier's standard lead time. This column should carry an INACTIVE relationship to Dim_Date[Date], activated only inside specific measures with USERELATIONSHIP when an expected-delivery-date view is genuinely needed.
QuantityOrderedWhole NumberUnits ordered on this line. Compared against Fact_StockReceipts[QuantityReceived] to calculate a Fill Rate % measure.
UnitCostNGNCurrency (Whole Number)Agreed unit cost for this order line in Naira, taken from the medication's current cost at order time.
TotalOrderValueNGNCurrency (Whole Number)QuantityOrdered multiplied by UnitCostNGN. Summed by the Total Purchase Order Value measure, the primary procurement spend KPI.
OrderStatusTextDelivered, Partially Delivered, Pending, Delayed or Cancelled. Drives the order-status donut chart and several fill-rate and on-time measures.
PaymentStatusTextPaid, Partially Paid, Pending or Overdue. Useful for a payables-management visual, mirroring the Collection Rate concept from the sales side of the business.
PaymentTermsUsedTextThe payment terms actually applied to this order, which may differ from the supplier's default PaymentTerms if a one-off arrangement was agreed.

Column Guide - Fact_StockReceipts

Records the actual physical receipt of stock, including the batch number and expiry date that are essential for pharmacy inventory management. Around 15% of Delivered/Partially Delivered orders generate more than one receipt row, modelling real-world split deliveries.

ColumnData TypeWhat it means / your task
ReceiptIDText (Key)Unique identifier for the receipt, formatted RCT000001. Primary key of Fact_StockReceipts.
POIDText (Foreign Key)Links the receipt back to Fact_PurchaseOrders[POID]. This fact-to-fact relationship is one-to-many because a single order can be delivered in more than one batch.
MedicationIDText (Foreign Key)Links the receipt directly to Dim_Medications[MedicationID], duplicating the medication reference already implied by the parent purchase order, which is normal star-schema practice and keeps this fact table independently filterable.
ReceiptDateDateThe date the batch physically arrived. Deliberately NOT related to Dim_Date in this model, since it is only ever used for reference alongside the purchase order's own OrderDate and ExpectedDeliveryDate; not every date column needs a relationship if it will not be independently trended or filtered.
QuantityReceivedWhole NumberUnits received in this batch. Summed and compared against QuantityOrdered on the parent order to calculate Fill Rate %.
BatchNumberTextA fictional batch/lot number. Essential in real pharmacy operations for traceability and recalls; included here for realism and drillthrough detail.
ExpiryDateDateThe expiry date printed on this specific batch. Used alongside today's date in a DAX measure to flag stock nearing expiry, an important pharmacy-specific analytical pattern.
QualityCheckStatusTextPassed, Pending or Failed. A receiving-quality-control field; a Failed status would in practice trigger a return to the supplier, connecting conceptually (though not directly modelled) to Fact_Returns.

Column Guide - Fact_Sales

The largest and most central fact table for the revenue side of the business. It records every sale across all retail and hospital pharmacy branches (warehouses do not sell directly to customers in this model) and drives the entire Sales & Revenue dashboard page.

ColumnData TypeWhat it means / your task
SaleIDText (Key)Unique identifier for the transaction, formatted SAL000001. Primary key of Fact_Sales.
BranchIDText (Foreign Key)Links the sale to the selling branch in Dim_Branches[BranchID]. Restricted to Retail Pharmacy and Hospital Pharmacy branch types in this dataset.
MedicationIDText (Foreign Key)Links the sale to Dim_Medications[MedicationID]. Every revenue-by-category and top-selling-medication visual depends on this column.
PrescriberIDText (Foreign Key, optional)Links the sale to the prescribing doctor in Dim_Prescribers, populated for roughly 85% of prescription-only medication sales and left blank for over-the-counter sales. Students must handle this optional relationship carefully, since a blank foreign key means the row simply will not appear when a report is filtered strictly by prescriber.
DispensingEmployeeIDText (Foreign Key)Links the sale to the Pharmacist or Pharmacy Technician in Dim_Employees who dispensed it. Used on the Top Dispensing Staff visual.
SaleDateDateThe date of the transaction. Carries an active relationship to Dim_Date[Date], the primary time axis for every revenue trend visual in the project.
QuantitySoldWhole NumberUnits sold in this transaction. Summed to build the Total Quantity Sold measure.
UnitSellingPriceNGNCurrency (Whole Number)The price per unit at the time of sale, copied from Dim_Medications[UnitSellingPriceNGN] at transaction time.
DiscountNGNCurrency (Whole Number)Any discount applied to this transaction, present on roughly 12% of sales. Subtracted when TotalSaleNGN is calculated.
TotalSaleNGNCurrency (Whole Number)QuantitySold multiplied by UnitSellingPriceNGN, minus DiscountNGN. The column summed by the Total Sales Revenue measure, the single most important KPI on the Sales & Revenue page.
PaymentMethodTextCash, POS/Card, Bank Transfer, Mobile Money or HMO Settlement. Useful for a payment-mix visual.
IsPrescriptionSaleText (Yes/No)Flags whether the sale required a prescription. Drives the Prescription Sales % measure.
CustomerTypeTextWalk-in, Corporate, Hospital Referral or Insurance/HMO. Useful for a customer-mix visual on the Sales & Revenue page.

Column Guide - Fact_StockTransfers

Records stock moving between two branches, most commonly from a distribution warehouse out to retail or hospital branches, but also occasionally between two retail branches for emergency rebalancing. This is the table behind the single most important DAX teaching moment in the whole project: Dim_Branches is related to it TWICE.

ColumnData TypeWhat it means / your task
TransferIDText (Key)Unique identifier for the transfer, formatted TRF000001. Primary key of Fact_StockTransfers.
FromBranchIDText (Foreign Key)Links to Dim_Branches[BranchID], identifying the sending branch. This relationship should be created ACTIVE, since most transfer reporting naturally asks 'which branch sent this stock'.
ToBranchIDText (Foreign Key)Also links to Dim_Branches[BranchID], identifying the receiving branch. Because Dim_Branches already has an active relationship to FromBranchID, this second relationship to the same table must be created INACTIVE, and activated inside specific measures using USERELATIONSHIP whenever a 'stock received via transfer' view is needed.
MedicationIDText (Foreign Key)Links the transfer to Dim_Medications[MedicationID]. Each transfer line is for a single medication SKU.
TransferDateDateThe date the transfer was executed. Carries an active relationship to Dim_Date[Date].
QuantityTransferredWhole NumberUnits moved in this transfer. Summed to build the Total Stock Transferred measure.
TransferReasonTextStock Rebalancing, Emergency Restock, New Branch Seeding, Expiry Risk Mitigation or Warehouse Redistribution. Useful for understanding why stock is moving around the network.
ApprovedByEmployeeIDText (Foreign Key)Links to the Store Manager or Inventory Officer in Dim_Employees who approved the transfer.
TransferStatusTextCompleted, In Transit or Cancelled. Useful for an operational visual showing transfers currently in progress.

Column Guide - Fact_Returns

Captures stock leaving the network for reasons other than a sale: expiry, damage, manufacturer recall, customer return or a negotiated overstock return to the supplier. This is the primary table behind the Inventory & Loss Management dashboard page.

ColumnData TypeWhat it means / your task
ReturnIDText (Key)Unique identifier for the return, formatted RET000001. Primary key of Fact_Returns.
BranchIDText (Foreign Key)Links the return to the branch it occurred at, via Dim_Branches[BranchID].
MedicationIDText (Foreign Key)Links the return to Dim_Medications[MedicationID].
ReturnDateDateThe date the return or write-off was recorded. Carries an active relationship to Dim_Date[Date].
QuantityWhole NumberUnits returned or written off. Summed to build a Total Units Lost measure.
ReturnReasonTextExpired, Damaged in Storage, Damaged in Transit, Recalled by Manufacturer, Customer Return or Overstock Return to Supplier. The field behind the Returns by Reason bar chart.
ValueNGNCurrency (Whole Number)Quantity multiplied by the medication's unit cost. Summed by the Total Loss Value measure, an important shrinkage/wastage KPI.
DisposalMethodTextIncineration, Return to Supplier, Landfill (Approved), Pending Disposal, or 'N/A - Returned to Stock' for customer returns that were resaleable. Useful for a compliance-style disposal-tracking visual.

4. DAX Measures To Be Built

Build the following measures, in this order, inside a dedicated Measures table. For each one, the goal (why it exists) is given - your task is to write the DAX yourself before checking the Solutions file.

Measure NameUsed OnYour Task (goal of the measure)
Total Purchase OrdersProcurement & Supplier PerformanceThe foundation activity KPI for the whole procurement side of the business.
Total Purchase Order ValueProcurement & Supplier Performance, Executive OverviewRepresents total procurement spend committed to suppliers, the starting point of the supply chain cost story.
Total Quantity OrderedProcurement & Supplier PerformanceThe denominator needed to calculate Fill Rate %, and a useful volume KPI in its own right.
Total Quantity ReceivedProcurement & Supplier PerformanceRepresents what physically arrived, as distinct from what was ordered - the two numbers should diverge for Partially Delivered and Delayed orders.
Fill Rate %Procurement & Supplier PerformanceA core procurement-quality KPI: a Fill Rate consistently below 100% for a given supplier signals a reliability problem worth escalating.
Average Supplier Lead Time (Days)Procurement & Supplier PerformanceGives a quick reference point for how long a supplier says it needs, to compare against the measures below that calculate how long it actually took.
On-Time Delivery %Procurement & Supplier PerformanceThe single most important supplier-reliability KPI in the project, and the clearest possible demonstration of why USERELATIONSHIP exists: without it, this measure would silently use the wrong date column.
Total Sales RevenueExecutive Overview, Sales & Revenue PerformanceThe single most important revenue KPI in the whole project.
Total Quantity SoldSales & Revenue PerformanceA volume KPI distinct from revenue, useful because a high-volume, low-cost medication and a low-volume, high-cost medication can generate similar revenue but represent very different operational realities.
Gross Margin NGNSales & Revenue PerformanceRevenue alone does not tell a pharmacy business whether it is actually profitable on a given medication or branch; this measure introduces true row-by-row profitability.
Gross Margin %Sales & Revenue PerformanceThe standard way to communicate profitability so it is comparable across a small branch and a large one.
Prescription Sales %Sales & Revenue PerformanceA useful regulatory and business-mix indicator: a pharmacy business heavily skewed toward prescription sales has a different risk and staffing profile than one skewed toward OTC retail.
Total Stock TransferredInventory & Loss ManagementThe base activity KPI for the inter-branch redistribution side of the supply chain.
Stock Received via TransferInventory & Loss ManagementThis measure is the clearest possible demonstration of why role-playing dimensions and USERELATIONSHIP matter: placed in a table with BranchName on rows, Total Stock Transferred shows what a branch sent out, while this measure shows what the same branch received in.
Total Loss ValueInventory & Loss ManagementA core shrinkage/wastage KPI. Using cost rather than selling price is deliberate, since a written-off unit was never sold and therefore never generated the margin embedded in the selling price.
Expired Stock Loss %Inventory & Loss ManagementExpiry is usually the single most controllable source of pharmacy shrinkage, since it is a direct function of ordering too much stock relative to sell-through speed; this measure isolates that specific driver.
Purchase Order Growth % (MoM)Executive OverviewTeaches the VAR/RETURN pattern for readable DAX and introduces time intelligence functions dependent on a properly configured date table, mirroring the equivalent admissions-growth measure from the hospital analytics project but applied to procurement spend.
Below Reorder Level FlagProcurement & Supplier PerformanceA simplified illustrative reorder-alert measure. Note this dataset does not model live stock-on-hand, so this measure compares period ordering volume against the reorder threshold as a teaching proxy, not as a substitute for a true point-in-time inventory balance, which is explicitly called out as a discussion point in the Solutions file.
