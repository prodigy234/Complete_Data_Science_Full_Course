How To Use This Guide

This is Part 1 of 2 of the training guide. It explains what every column in the workbook means, what is wrong with the raw data, and exactly what you are being asked to do about it - first in Power Query (data cleaning), then in Power BI (relationships, DAX measures and dashboards). Attempt every task yourself in Nigerian_Breweries_PowerQuery_PowerBI_Training_Data.xlsx before opening PART 2 - Solutions.

DAX Measures To Be Built

For each table below, your task is to write a DAX measure that achieves the stated purpose. Do not look at the solutions guide yet - try writing the DAX yourself first using SUM, CALCULATE, DIVIDE, COUNTROWS and related functions.

Operations

  • Total Volume Produced (HL) - Sum of all batch volumes actually produced.
  • Total Target Volume (HL) - Sum of all planned/target volumes.
  • Production Efficiency % - Actual volume produced as a percentage of target volume.
  • Total Downtime Hours - Sum of downtime hours across all batches.
  • Quality Pass Rate % - Share of batches with a QualityStatus of Pass.

Supply_Chain

  • Total Procurement Spend (NGN) - Sum of quantity ordered multiplied by unit cost.
  • On-Time Delivery % - Share of purchase orders delivered On Time.
  • Average Lead Time (Days) - Average number of days between order date and delivery date.
  • Total Open POs - Count of purchase orders not yet delivered.

Legal

  • Total Active Contract Value (NGN) - Sum of contract value for contracts that are not expired.
  • Contracts Expiring Next 90 Days - Count of contracts expiring in the next 90 days, for renewal planning.
  • Breach Case Count - Count of cases flagged as Breach Identified.

Admin

  • Total Asset Value (NGN) - Sum of all tracked asset values.
  • Assets Needing Maintenance - Count of assets Due for Maintenance or Under Repair.
  • Estimated Annual Depreciation (NGN) - Sum of asset value multiplied by its depreciation rate.

Customer_Service

  • Total Tickets - Count of all customer service tickets logged.
  • Average Resolution Time (Hours) - Average time to resolve a ticket.
  • Average Satisfaction Score - Average post-resolution satisfaction score, ignoring blanks by design.
  • Escalation Rate % - Share of tickets marked Escalated.

IT

  • Total IT Tickets - Count of all IT service desk tickets.
  • Critical Ticket Count - Count of tickets flagged Critical priority, after priority is standardised.
  • Average IT Resolution Time (Hours) - Average resolution time across IT tickets.
  • Total IT Support Cost (NGN) - Sum of support cost across tickets that had a direct cost.

Accounts_Finance

  • Total Revenue (NGN) - Sum of Amount for Revenue-type transactions.
  • Total Operating Expense (NGN) - Sum of Amount for Operating Expense transactions.
  • Net Position (NGN) - Revenue less operating expense, a simplified training margin figure.
  • Total Payroll Cost (NGN) - Sum of Amount for Payroll-type transactions.

Human_Resources

  • Headcount (Active) - Count of employees with EmploymentStatus = Active, from Dim_Employees.
  • Total Training Spend (NGN) - Sum of Cost for Training-type HR records.
  • Attrition Count - Count of Exit-type HR records in the period.
  • Average Performance Rating - Average of the latest performance rating across active employees.

Internal_Control

  • Open Findings - Count of audit findings not yet closed.
  • Overdue Findings - Count of findings past their due date and still open.
  • Critical Findings - Count of findings tagged Critical severity, after severity is standardised.

Risk_Management

  • Average Risk Score - Average of Likelihood x Impact across the risk register.
  • High Priority Open Risks - Count of risks with a recalculated score of 15 or more that are not yet mitigated.
  • Risks Mitigated % - Share of logged risks marked Mitigated.
