1. Dataset column dictionary

Before touching Power BI, work through every table below and understand each column - what it means, its correct data type, and how it will be used. This is the foundation everything else in this training is built on.

Dim_Date Dimension

Rows: 1,461 (2023-01-01 to 2026-12-31, one row per day)  |  Model role: Standalone calendar table. Mark as a Date Table in Power BI. Every fact table connects to it.

ColumnData typeDescription
DateDateOne calendar day. The primary key of the table and the field most relationships are built on.
YearWhole NumberCalendar year (2023-2026). Used to slice every dashboard by year and build year-over-year measures.
QuarterNumberWhole NumberQuarter as a number (1-4). Sort-by column for the Quarter text field.
QuarterTextQuarter label such as Q1, Q2. Sorted by QuarterNumber so it does not sort alphabetically.
MonthNumberWhole NumberMonth as a number (1-12). Sort-by column for MonthName/MonthShort.
MonthNameTextFull month name. Used on axis labels where space allows.
MonthShortTextAbbreviated month name (Jan, Feb...). Used on compact chart axes.
MonthYearTextMonth and year combined (Jan-2023). Used as the axis field on multi-year trend charts.
WeekNumberWhole NumberISO week number (1-53). Supports weekly training-activity analysis.
DayWhole NumberDay of month (1-31).
DayNameTextFull weekday name. Used to analyse which weekdays sessions are run on.
DayOfWeekNumberWhole NumberWeekday as a number, Monday=1...Sunday=7. Sort-by column for DayName.
IsWeekendTrue/FalseTRUE for Saturday/Sunday. Used to flag weekend session delivery.
IsWorkingDayTrue/FalseOpposite of IsWeekend.

Dim_Locations Dimension

Rows: 25 global offices across 5 regions  |  Model role: The geography dimension. Connects to Dim_Employees (where staff are based) and to Fact_Sessions (where training is delivered).

ColumnData typeDescription
LocationIDTextUnique key, format LOC001. Relationship key to Dim_Employees and Fact_Sessions.
CityTextOffice city.
CountryTextOffice country.
RegionTextOne of 5 regions: North America, Latin America, Europe, Middle East, Africa, Asia Pacific. Used for regional roll-ups and the map visual.
OfficeTypeTextHQ, Regional Hub or Branch. Used to compare training reach between headquarters and smaller branch offices.

Dim_Employees Dimension (with a self-referencing hierarchy)

Rows: 180 employees across 10 business units and 25 offices  |  Model role: The staffing dimension. ManagerID refers back to EmployeeID in the same table, building a parent-child org hierarchy - a distinct relationship pattern from a normal dimension-to-fact link.

ColumnData typeDescription
EmployeeIDTextUnique key, format EMP0001. Relationship key from Fact_Enrollments and Fact_Feedback, and the 'one' side of the self-referencing hierarchy.
FullNameTextEmployee display name.
BusinessUnitTextOne of 10 business units (Finance, HR, IT, Sales & Commercial, Marketing, Operations & Supply Chain, Legal & Compliance, Customer Service, R&D, Manufacturing).
JobLevelTextStaff, Senior Staff, Manager, Senior Manager, Director or Executive. Drives eligibility for leadership programs and the org hierarchy depth.
LocationIDTextForeign key to Dim_Locations - where the employee is based.
HireDateDateDate the employee joined. Used to compute tenure and to check onboarding-program timing.
EmploymentStatusTextActive or Inactive. Used to exclude former staff from current training-completion measures.
ManagerIDTextEmployeeID of this employee's manager, blank for the most senior Executives. The self-referencing relationship key used to build 'Direct Reports' measures and org-hierarchy visuals.

Dim_Trainers Dimension

Rows: 42 trainers (internal facilitators and external vendors)  |  Model role: One row per trainer/facilitator. Connects to Fact_Sessions.

ColumnData typeDescription
TrainerIDTextUnique key, format TR001. Relationship key to Fact_Sessions.
TrainerNameTextTrainer display name.
TrainerTypeTextInternal or External Vendor. Used to compare cost and rating between in-house and outsourced delivery.
SpecializationTextThe specific subject the trainer delivers, e.g. Data Privacy & GDPR, Executive Coaching.
TrainingTypeFocusTextWhich of the four training types (Compliance, Onboarding, L&D, Leadership) this trainer mainly delivers.

Dim_TrainingPrograms Dimension

Rows: 27 training programs across 4 training types  |  Model role: The course catalogue. Connects to Fact_Sessions and Fact_Enrollments.

ColumnData typeDescription
ProgramIDTextUnique key, format PRG001. Relationship key to Fact_Sessions and Fact_Enrollments.
ProgramNameTextCourse title, e.g. Data Privacy & GDPR Essentials, Strategic Leadership Program.
TrainingTypeTextCompliance, Onboarding, L&D or Leadership. The main way the whole training catalogue is sliced.
DeliveryModeTextIn-Person, Virtual Live, Self-Paced or Blended.
ProviderTypeTextInternal or External Vendor - who owns/develops the course content.
DurationHoursDecimal NumberLength of the course in hours. Used to compute total training hours delivered.
MandatoryText (Yes/No)Whether the course is compulsory. All Compliance and Onboarding courses are mandatory by design.
TargetAudienceTextWho the course is intended for, e.g. All Staff, New Hires, Managers & Above, Directors & Above.
CostPerParticipant_USDDecimal NumberBudgeted cost per participant, used with session attendance to estimate delivered cost.

Fact_Sessions Fact (grain: one delivered session/cohort)

Rows: 254 rows  |  Model role: One row per training session actually run. Sits between the course catalogue and individual enrollments, and is itself a parent of Fact_Enrollments.

ColumnData typeDescription
SessionIDTextUnique key for the session. Relationship key to Fact_Enrollments.
ProgramIDTextForeign key to Dim_TrainingPrograms - which course this session delivers.
TrainerIDTextForeign key to Dim_Trainers - who delivered the session.
LocationIDTextForeign key to Dim_Locations - where the session was held.
SessionDateDateDate the session ran. Foreign key to Dim_Date.
ModalityTextHow the session was actually delivered: In-Person, Virtual Live or Self-Paced.
CapacityWhole NumberMaximum seats available for the session.
ActualAttendanceWhole NumberNumber of employees who actually attended. Compared against Capacity for the attendance-rate measure.
SessionCostUSDDecimal NumberActual delivered cost of the session (trainer fees, materials, venue where applicable).

Fact_Enrollments Fact (grain: one employee enrolled in one session)

Rows: 5,993 rows  |  Model role: The most granular and most important table in the model - every training completion, compliance deadline and certification expiry lives here.

ColumnData typeDescription
EnrollmentIDTextUnique key for the enrollment record.
EmployeeIDTextForeign key to Dim_Employees - who enrolled.
ProgramIDTextForeign key to Dim_TrainingPrograms - which course.
SessionIDTextForeign key to Fact_Sessions - which specific session/cohort.
EnrollmentDateDateDate the employee enrolled. The default active relationship to Dim_Date.
DueDateDateCompliance/onboarding completion deadline, blank for optional L&D and Leadership courses. An inactive relationship to Dim_Date, activated with USERELATIONSHIP.
CompletionDateDateDate the employee actually completed the course, blank if not yet completed. Also an inactive relationship to Dim_Date.
StatusTextNot Started, In Progress, Completed or Failed.
ScoreDecimal NumberAssessment score, populated for Compliance courses that end in a quiz, blank otherwise.
CertificationExpiryDateDateFor completed Compliance enrollments, the date the certification lapses and must be renewed (one year after completion), blank otherwise.
CompletionMethodTextHow the training was delivered/consumed for this enrollment: In-Person, Virtual Live or Self-Paced.

Fact_Feedback Fact (grain: one post-training survey response)

Rows: 3,755 rows  |  Model role: Post-training evaluation scores. Links back to Fact_Enrollments on EnrollmentID (a one-to-one relationship) as well as directly to Dim_Employees and Dim_TrainingPrograms.

ColumnData typeDescription
FeedbackIDTextUnique key for the feedback record.
EnrollmentIDTextForeign key to Fact_Enrollments - the specific enrollment this feedback relates to. One enrollment has at most one feedback row (one-to-one).
EmployeeIDTextForeign key to Dim_Employees - who submitted the feedback.
ProgramIDTextForeign key to Dim_TrainingPrograms - which course the feedback is about.
SubmittedDateDateDate the survey was submitted. Foreign key to Dim_Date.
SatisfactionScoreWhole NumberOverall satisfaction, 1 (low) to 5 (high).
TrainerRatingWhole NumberRating of the trainer/facilitator, 1 to 5.
ContentRelevanceScoreWhole NumberHow relevant the content was to the employee's role, 1 to 5.
WouldRecommendText (Yes/No)Whether the employee would recommend the course to a colleague.

Fact_Budget Fact (grain: one region/training-type/month)

Rows: 1,056 rows  |  Model role: Planned vs actual training spend by region, training type and month.

ColumnData typeDescription
BudgetLineIDTextUnique key for the budget line.
RegionTextOne of the 5 regions from Dim_Locations. Note: joined to Dim_Locations by value, not by a location key - see Section 4 for how this is modelled.
TrainingTypeTextCompliance, Onboarding, L&D or Leadership.
MonthDateFirst day of the month the budget line applies to. Foreign key to Dim_Date.
PlannedBudget_USDDecimal NumberBudgeted spend for that region/training type/month.
ActualSpend_USDDecimal NumberActual spend incurred, compared against PlannedBudget_USD for variance.

2. Data loading tasks (Power Query)

The Excel workbook is already clean - your job is to load all nine sheets correctly, set the right data types, and prepare the model. Attempt each task below yourself before checking the Solutions HTML file.

Task 1 - Dim_Date

Load the sheet, confirm Date is typed as Date (not Date/Time), and mark this table as a Date Table on the Date column.

Task 2 - Dim_Locations

Load the sheet and confirm every column is typed as Text.

Task 3 - Dim_Employees

Load the sheet, confirm HireDate is typed as Date, and leave ManagerID as Text even though some values are blank.

Task 4 - Dim_Trainers

Load the sheet and confirm every column is typed as Text.

Task 5 - Dim_TrainingPrograms

Load the sheet and set DurationHours and CostPerParticipant_USD to the correct numeric types.

Task 6 - Fact_Sessions

Load the sheet, set SessionDate to Date, and Capacity/ActualAttendance/SessionCostUSD to the correct numeric types.

Task 7 - Fact_Enrollments

Load the sheet, set all four date columns correctly, and confirm Score and CertificationExpiryDate are allowed to be blank.

Task 8 - Fact_Feedback

Load the sheet, set SubmittedDate to Date, and the three score columns to Whole Number.

Task 9 - Fact_Budget

Load the sheet and set Month to Date and the two cost columns to Decimal Number.

Think about it: Dim_Date connects to Fact_Enrollments three different ways (EnrollmentDate, DueDate, CompletionDate). Why can Power BI only make one of these active at a time, and what DAX function lets a measure use one of the inactive ones anyway? And separately - Dim_Employees has a relationship pointing back at itself (EmployeeID to ManagerID). What real-world structure does that model, and what DAX function would you use to count a manager's direct reports? Work out your answers, then confirm them in the Solutions file and the Word manual.
  1. Build the core one-to-many relationships: Dim_Locations to Dim_Employees and Fact_Sessions; Dim_Trainers to Fact_Sessions; Dim_TrainingPrograms to Fact_Sessions, Fact_Enrollments and Fact_Feedback; Fact_Sessions to Fact_Enrollments.
  2. Build the self-referencing relationship inside Dim_Employees, from EmployeeID (one) to ManagerID (many), to enable the org hierarchy.
  3. Connect Dim_Date to Fact_Enrollments[EnrollmentDate], Fact_Sessions[SessionDate], Fact_Feedback[SubmittedDate] and Fact_Budget[Month] as the four active date relationships.
  4. Add the two additional Fact_Enrollments date relationships (DueDate and CompletionDate) and leave them inactive - Power BI will grey these out automatically once EnrollmentDate is active.
  5. Build the one-to-one relationship between Fact_Enrollments[EnrollmentID] and Fact_Feedback[EnrollmentID], and confirm Power BI reports its cardinality as One-to-One in the relationship properties panel.
  6. Confirm every one-to-many relationship reads One-to-Many with the 1 on the dimension side, and cross filter direction is Single, before moving on to the DAX measures.

4. DAX measures to build

For every measure below, write the DAX yourself first. Only the measure name and what it should return/drive are given here - the full formula, line-by-line explanation and dashboard impact are in the Solutions HTML file and the Word manual.

  1. Create a dedicated Measures table (a blank table with no columns from any source) to hold every DAX measure, keeping the model organised.
  2. Build the 7 foundational measures (Total Employees, Total Enrollments, Employees Trained, Overall Completion Rate %, Total Training Hours Delivered, Total Training Spend, Average Satisfaction Score).
  3. Build the 6 compliance measures, including the two USERELATIONSHIP measures for DueDate and CompletionDate.
  4. Build the 5 program & session measures, including the RANKX-based Program Enrollment Rank.
  5. Build the 5 budget & cost measures, including the DATEADD time-intelligence measure - this requires Dim_Date to already be marked as a Date Table.
  6. Build the 5 employee development & feedback measures, including the RELATEDTABLE-based Direct Reports measure that uses the self-referencing hierarchy.
